## 1. Purpose

This One Point Lesson explains controlled temperature ramp-up procedure for YD-2301 (POLYMER FLUID BED DRYER) in the POWDER DRYING SYSTEM. Heating the drum too quickly warps the shell and cracks the steam tubes, causing tube leaks and long downtime. The lesson gives operators and technicians the key knowledge and the correct step-by-step method so the task is done safely, correctly and consistently every time.

## 2. Safety precautions

Hazard note: this equipment is HIGH CRITICAL handling Polymer powder / LP Steam at up to 6 barg (shell) / 150 degC.

- Obtain the correct operating permit and brief the shift team before the task.
- Confirm hazardous fluids are isolated / inerted as required by procedure.
- Beware of flammable, hot, or high-pressure fluids; wear the correct PPE.
- Never defeat a safety interlock or trip without an authorised override permit.
- Have emergency actions and communications ready before starting.

## 4. Procedure

| Step | Action | Check / acceptance (as printed) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ramp TIC-2301 at a maximum of 10 degC per 10 minutes | Permit valid & briefed |
| 2 | Hold at 60 degC to let the drum warm through evenly | Parameter within limit |
| 3 | Watch drum thermal growth at the trunnion supports | Interlock status confirmed |
| 4 | Reach the 95-110 degC operating band slowly | Change logged on DCS |
| 5 | If uneven expansion / rubbing is heard, pause the ramp | Handover to next shift |

## 6. Key learning points

- Heating the drum too quickly warps the shell and cracks the steam tubes, causing tube leaks and long downtime.
- Respect the interlock SEQ-5500: e.g. TSHH-2301 trips at > 125 degC - never defeat it without a permit.
- Always cross-check the Datasheet limits and the P&ID TJC-LLD-PID-2301 before working on YD-2301.
